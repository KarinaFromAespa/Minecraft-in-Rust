# Roadmap — where 1.21.8 fits

This directory is the 1.21.8 rewrite of the
[Minecraft-in-Rust](../../README.md) programme — the third adjacent delta
port in the Components era (E6), taken from 1.21.9 (protocol 773 → 772).
The programme's plan — era decomposition, the
per-version pipeline, sequencing and gates — is in
[docs/ROADMAP.md](../../docs/ROADMAP.md) at the repository root, and every
version's status is indexed in [VERSIONS.md](../../VERSIONS.md).

For this version specifically, [PORT-NOTES-1.21.8.md](PORT-NOTES-1.21.8.md)
is the record: what the port has done, its parity profile, and what remains.

This file previously held the upstream horizon-rust project's roadmap. It
is not reproduced here: this programme is independent of that project, and
its plan lives in the document linked above.
